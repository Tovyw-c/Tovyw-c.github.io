Privacy Policy

Cosmic Clicker: Planet Breaker (Android) · Developer: TOVYW · Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game "Cosmic Clicker: Planet Breaker" (the "Game") uses, why, and what choices you have. The Game does not require an account, has no in-app purchases and we do not sell your data.

1. Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device. We do not receive them. They are deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its data. If Android backup is turned on in your phone settings, Android may include this data in your device backup to your Google account; this is handled by Google, not by us.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows optional rewarded ads (you choose to watch an ad to get a bonus). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix errors, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address (which can indicate your approximate location),
  • device and app information (for example phone model, Android version, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads and diagnostic (crash and performance) data.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the Game asks for your consent (Google User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the Game: Settings > AD PRIVACY. Elsewhere you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Google > Ads). Ad content in the Game is limited to content suitable for teens.

Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Feedback

If you use Settings > FEEDBACK, the Game opens your e-mail app with a message to us. If you send it, we receive your e-mail address, your message and technical details added to it (game version, phone model, Android version and game language). We use them only to answer you and to fix problems, and we delete them when they are no longer needed or when you ask us to.

4. Notifications

The Game can show local notifications (for example when your offline rewards are ready). They are scheduled on your device and no data is sent to us. You can turn them off in your Android settings.

5. Rating prompt

The Game may once ask you to rate it using Google Play In-App Review. Your rating and review are handled by Google Play under Google's terms.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data through feedback, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of the personal data we hold about you (in practice only feedback e-mails). For data processed by Google for advertising, use the consent option in the Game, your Android ad settings, or contact Google. If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Poland: Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address, with the effective date at the top.

9. Contact

TOVYW · cosmicclickergame@gmail.com

2. Reklamy (Google AdMob)

Gra pokazuje dobrowolne reklamy z nagrodą (sam decydujesz, czy obejrzeć reklamę, żeby dostać bonus). Reklamy dostarcza Google AdMob. Aby wyświetlać i mierzyć reklamy, zapobiegać oszustwom i naprawiać błędy, pakiet Google Mobile Ads może zbierać i przetwarzać:

  • identyfikator reklamowy urządzenia,

  • informacje o urządzeniu i aplikacji (np. model telefonu, wersja Androida, język, wersja gry),

Wybór możesz zmienić w każdej chwili w Grze: Settings > AD PRIVACY. W ustawieniach Androida (Google > Reklamy) możesz też zresetować lub usunąć identyfikator reklamowy albo wyłączyć reklamy spersonalizowane. Treści reklam w Grze są ograniczone do odpowiednich dla nastolatków.

Dane wysyłane do Google są szyfrowane podczas przesyłania. Więcej: Jak Google wykorzystuje informacje z aplikacji korzystających z jego usług oraz Polityka prywatności Google.
